## Age Rating

### Recommended Rating: **12+**

### Rationale:
- **Infrequent/Mild Mature/Suggestive Themes**: No
- **Infrequent/Mild Profanity or Crude Humor**: Yes (Cosmic Roasts feature)
- **Infrequent/Mild Cartoon or Fantasy Violence**: No
- **Simulated Gambling**: No (tracking only, no actual trading)
- **Unrestricted Web Access**: No
- **Alcohol, Tobacco, or Drug Use**: No

### Why NOT 17+:
- App does not execute trades or connect to brokerages
- No real money transactions within the app (except subscription)
- Stock tracking is informational only
- Simulated Gambling does not apply — users are tracking real holdings, not simulating trades
- Entertainment disclaimer clearly states content is not financial advice

### Alternative Consideration:
If Apple reviewers express concern about finance content, be prepared to argue that:
1. The app is primarily entertainment (astrology-based)
2. No trades are executed through the app
3. Clear disclaimers are present throughout
4. Similar portfolio trackers are rated 4+ or 12+

2. NOT FINANCIAL ADVICE
The app prominently displays "Not Financial Advice" disclaimers throughout:
- On the main tab bar
- In the NFA Disclaimer screen (accessible from Settings)
- In the App Store description
- Within horoscope content

3. NO TRADING FUNCTIONALITY
The app does NOT:
- Execute trades
- Connect to brokerage accounts
- Handle real money (except subscription purchases)
- Provide buy/sell recommendations

Real-time stock quotes are provided by Finnhub.io API. Data may be delayed up to 15 minutes for free tier users. We clearly attribute this data source in the app.

The zodiac compatibility, horoscopes, moon phases, and all astrological content are entertainment features inspired by traditional astrology. There is no claim that these features can predict market movements. They are designed to add a fun, personalized layer to portfolio tracking.
